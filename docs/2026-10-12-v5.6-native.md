## English

Windows **v5.6.0-win.2** and macOS **v5.6.0-mac.2** were published on 2026-10-12. Download one EXE or DMG for your platform. The runtime, seven calculators, equipment templates and offline tutorial are bundled. Public releases contain the application and `SHA256SUMS.txt`; full acceptance ZIPs remain build artifacts.

The table above records the exact public filenames and SHA256 values. The linked native runs completed their build, second-platform verification and publish jobs successfully. Windows tested source commit `96cb26a7bf4f61b0f01a4f02024a89face4995bd`; macOS tested `a9f186506d2c28b169349aa05d650dc48986a414`. Source calculations, the existing 100-case stress evidence, and native delivered-app acceptance have separate scopes. See the [V5.6 feature and source record](2026-10-12-v5.6-expansion.md#english).

Native acceptance used Windows Server 2022/2025 x64 and Apple Silicon/Intel macOS 15. Windows 10/11 x64 and macOS 11+ remain support/packaging targets. A successful runner test does not certify every user device, keyboard, enterprise policy or engineering design.

The Windows EXE is unsigned; the Mac app has ad-hoc integrity signing without Developer ID/notarization. Follow the [security-prompt guide](signing.en.md). Existing releases, history, author attribution and licensing remain. Results are preliminary engineering estimates requiring site and manufacturer review.
