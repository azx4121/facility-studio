- Preserve old applications, projects, previews and historical releases. No automatic destructive migration.

## 來源變更範圍 / Source changes

- 工程狀態、過期來源、無效輸入與匯出護鎖；中英報告／規範文字及 DI 持續循環量範本公式與 U 說明。Engineering states, stale-source/input/export guards; bilingual reports/specifications and DI continuous-circulation template formula and U wording.
- C2 不可行傳遞、BF／ADP 風量搜尋、無效冰水數值建議修正。C2 infeasibility propagation, BF/ADP airflow search and removal of misleading numeric chilled-water remedies.
- 真空抽速必要條件、NP／泵輸入電力邊界、液體相態／物性與泵吸入覆核。Necessary vacuum speed, NP/pump input boundaries, liquid property/phase and suction review.
- 獨立 PV／液體入口、完整空氣線圖、直接照度、容量／擴建與設計資料審查。Independent PV/liquid entry points, full air charts, direct lighting, capacity/expansion and design-document review.
- 正式身份、來源 manifest／驗收 fail-closed 契約與舊發布管線退役。Formal identity, fail-closed manifest/acceptance contract and retirement of legacy release routes.
