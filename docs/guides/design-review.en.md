# Design basis and delivery review | 5.7.3

[繁體中文](design-review.md) · [Guides](README.en.md) · [Release status](../verification/5.7.3-release-acceptance.md)

This is a 5.7.3 version guide. Native acceptance and publication status are established by CI records and Release attachments for the corresponding commit; this guide does not assert a pass. This workflow supports document preparation within stated FEED scope; it does not certify engineering compliance.

1. Open **Design basis and delivery review** and create an independent project. Enter project/revision, site, owner, system scope/interfaces, design/worst-case conditions, units/reference states, acceptance criteria/tolerances and preparer.
2. Enter each specification as five fields: **name | edition | clause | applicable scope | source**. Use traceable project-specific references, not an unsupported generic claim of compliance.
3. Review the delivery register: basis, balances/demands, process/interfaces, equipment specifications, piping/materials, electrical/protection, controls, layout, risk and quantity/cost evidence. Mark scope and missing items honestly; the app does not generate all those engineering deliverables.
4. Explicitly attach the relevant documents. Attachments are copies at attachment time, not live links. If the source changes, attach the new revision and review again. Moving the project requires the entire folder, including evidence.
5. Record a reviewer different from the preparer. The application checks recorded names and review validity, not real identity, qualifications or cryptographic signatures. Design-basis/register/evidence changes invalidate prior review.
6. Save/export to new files, preserve previous revisions, and read the pending/failed items. “Documents complete, ready for engineering review” means the entered evidence and review records are complete, not that the project passed FEED or a regulator approved it.

Review calculations with vendor data at matching conditions, complete scope and a qualified project reviewer. Do not upload confidential attachments with a public bug report; reproduce problems using synthetic data.
